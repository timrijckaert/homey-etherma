ETHERMA's eTOUCH-PRO turns electric floor heating into comfort on demand. This app connects the thermostat to Homey, so warm floors greet you in the morning, the heating winds down when nobody is home, and your home follows the rhythm of your household.

Adjust the target temperature, switch heating modes and let Flows react to what the thermostat measures. Whether it is a warm bathroom floor on a winter morning or keeping the energy bill in check, your floor heating quietly does the right thing.
